\section{Real Charts: Predicting Errors and Their Type}
\label{sec:chartqa}

\paragraph{Will the answer be wrong? (E1, E2).} A sanity probe first confirmed
that each cache decodes what it plainly contains (human-written vs.\ augmented
question: validation AUROC 1.000 for Qwen, 0.999 for LLaVA). The binary probe
then predicts wrong answers far above the surface baseline in both models
(Table~\ref{tab:e1}), always at the last prompt token (Qwen layer 21 of 28,
LLaVA 24 of 32); image-token positions stay near the surface baseline at every
depth (Appendix~\ref{app:layers}). Removing the \cls{not\_an\_error} items raises
Qwen's probe from 0.877 to 0.932: part of its apparent error was label noise.
The signal is not question style. It holds within human-written questions only
(0.895 Qwen, clean; 0.850 LLaVA), regressing the surface features out lowers it
by only 0.02 to 0.07, and it moves by about $\pm$0.02 across five figure splits
(the reported Qwen split lies above the five-split mean of 0.910, clean).
Applied unchanged to 960 human-written validation questions on charts never
used in training, Qwen's probe scores 0.772 [0.73, 0.81]. Its probabilities are
calibrated (expected calibration error 0.03 for Qwen, 0.05 for LLaVA).

\paragraph{Misreads vs.\ arithmetic errors (E3).} ChartQA supplies enough
misreads and computation errors, but not fabrications, to train type probes
(Table~\ref{tab:chartqa-types}). Misreads are detectable well above the surface
features (0.892 and 0.813), and misreads and computation errors are separable
(0.897 and 0.961). In LLaVA each type probe barely responds to the other type
(0.586 and 0.540); Qwen's misread probe also flags computation errors (0.820),
but surface features alone reach 0.765 on that contrast. Computation errors only
occur on questions that ask for an operation, and that wording is visible from
the first layer (LLaVA's computation probe already scores 0.865 at layer~0). We
therefore tagged each question as \emph{arithmetic} or \emph{retrieval} with a
keyword rule fixed in advance and retrained within one type: the computation
probe still reaches 0.845 and 0.836 within arithmetic questions, and the misread
probe 0.913 and 0.805 within retrieval questions. Residualised, all three probes
stay between 0.80 and 0.92 in both models. On real charts, then, the two error
types the models actually make leave different, linearly readable signatures.
