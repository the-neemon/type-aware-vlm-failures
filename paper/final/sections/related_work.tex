\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Pre-generation failure detection.} Models often know when they are
likely to be wrong: language models' own estimates of correctness are informative
\citep{kadavath2022know}, and hallucination risk in VLMs can be read from internal
representations in a single forward pass, at up to 0.93 AUROC across eight
models \citep{kogilathota2026halp}. HALP also finds that the most informative
features differ by architecture, with \qwen best served by visual-only features.
\citet{zhang2026vibprobe} isolate hallucination-relevant attention heads with a
variational information bottleneck and steer with probe gradients. Both address
binary object hallucination on photographs; neither asks whether different kinds
of failure are internally distinguishable.

\paragraph{Failure types inside the model.} \citet{liu2026dualpathway} find, by
activation patching across five VLMs, a visual grounding pathway behind correct
predictions and a separate pathway driving errors, at different depths; the
circuit transfers to relational but not attribute hallucination. This
selectivity is the closest prior evidence that hallucination is not one
phenomenon, and it motivates probing every layer.

\paragraph{Chart reasoning and error taxonomies.} ChartQA
\citep{masry2022chartqa} is the standard chart question answering benchmark.
RADAR \citep{rani2025radar} and DRAGON \citep{iyengar2026dragon} annotate the
regions that justify an answer; they label \emph{where} a model should look, not
\emph{how} it failed. \citet{ashurytahan2026errormap} build failure taxonomies
for text-only LLMs with an LLM judge; we adopt the method, not the taxonomy.
Visual Self-Refine \citep{li2026visualselfrefine} makes a chart parser
re-examine its own localisations. Re-looking is thus established; whether the
right repair depends on the failure type is not.

\paragraph{Probes and selective prediction.} Linear probes read information
from intermediate layers \citep{alain2016probes}, but a probe can succeed by
exploiting properties of the input rather than of the model, so it needs
controls \citep{hewitt2019control}. \citet{sahoo2026linearprobes} show reasoning
type probes at 100\% accuracy collapsing to chance once format confounds are
residualised out, and \citet{yuan2026hiddenerror} find a hidden-state error
probe at 0.95 AUROC whose signal supported no successful intervention. We
therefore report every probe beside an activation-free baseline and test
repairs directly. We evaluate abstention with risk-coverage curves, as in
selective classification \citep{geifman2017selective}.
