Failure types come from the answers (`src/synth/items.py`): structural is a
misread of a shown bar; fabrication is a number given for a missing bar; zero
is "0" for a missing bar, which the team treats as a refusal expressed as a
number. A cell is P(recovered): the repaired answer is correct, or for a
missing bar, a rejection.

## Read these before writing them up

1. **For a missing bar, I_crop is I_upsample.** There is no region to crop, so
   the repair shows the whole figure (the rows carry the note "named category
   absent; whole figure shown"), and the two give identical answers on every
   missing-bar item. The fabrication side of the primary test therefore
   measures upsampling. The crop's own gain on misreads, beyond the extra
   pixels, is the I_crop vs I_upsample row: clear for LLaVA (+0.145), and for
   Qwen positive but with an interval touching 0 (46 misreads).
2. **LLaVA's argmax "flip" is not evidence.** Every LLaVA fabrication cell is 0,
   so its best repair for fabrication is a tie broken by name, and the 1.00
   stability reflects that tie. For LLaVA the supported claim is narrower:
   cropping rescues misreads, and nothing rescues missing-bar answers, which
   leaves abstention as the only repair for them (E5).
3. **Qwen's flip is supported.** Its fabrications are fixed only by asking it to
   verify (13 of 91 become rejections), its misreads mainly by cropping, with
   stability 0.99.
4. **I_verify turns Qwen's "0" answers into rejections** (342 of 494), which
   supports reading "0" as a refusal expressed as a number. LLaVA instead turns
   most of its "0" answers into invented numbers under I_reask (215 of 232) and
   I_verify (165).
5. Qwen has only 46 misreads, so its structural cells are wide.
